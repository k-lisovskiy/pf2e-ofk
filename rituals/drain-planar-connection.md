# Overview

- **Rank**: 5
- **Cast**: 1 day
- **Primary Check**: DC 31 Nature (master)
- **Secondary Casters**: 5
- **Secondary Checks**: DC 26 Diplomacy or Nature or Occultism
- **Cost**: six sets of rare herbs, each worth a total of 50 gp

## Description

You draw the planar energy from Idyllis, through the ley lines, and into the six latticework spheres.

---

**Critical Success** You sever Idyllis's connection to the Plane of Wood permanently.

**Success** You sever Idyllis's connection to the Plane of Wood for 10 years.

**Failure** You sever Idyllis's connection to the Plane of Wood for 1 year, but the ritual backfires, dealing (4d12+26) force damage to all secondary casters (reflex save (DC 26) save).

**Critical Failure** The ritual backfires as a failure but doesn't sever Idyllis's connection.

# Overview

- **Rank**: 3
- **Cast**: reaction
- **Range**: 60 feet
- **Targets**: the creature targeted by the attack
- **Duration**: 1 round
- **Traditions**: arcane, primal
- **Traits**: air, manipulate

## Description

**Trigger** You or an ally in range is targeted by a ranged attack.

---

A cloud of mist enshrouds the target, appearing much like the deflecting clouds created by cloud dragons. The target is treated as Hidden for the purposes of resolving the triggering attack (so normally the attacker must succeed at a flat check (DC 11) to target it) and all ranged attacks against it for the duration.

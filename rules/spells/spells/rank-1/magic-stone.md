# Overview

- **Rank**: 1
- **Cast**: 1 to 3
- **Range**: touch
- **Targets**: 1 to 3 non-magical stones or sling bullets
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Traditions**: divine, primal
- **Traits**: concentrate, manipulate, vitality

## Description

You pour vitality energy into ordinary stones, granting them temporary magical properties. You can target 1 non-magical stone or sling bullet for every action you use Casting this Spell. The stones must be unattended or carried by you or a willing ally. The stones become *+1 striking disrupting Sling Bullets*. Each stone can be used only once, after which it crumbles to dust.

## Description

You chant a marching song of Yled to empower the undead and terrify the living. Undead creatures within the area gain the Quickened condition for 1 minute, which they can use only to Stride.

Living creatures in the area must attempt a Will save. This gains the emotion, fear, and mental traits for living creatures.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is Frightened 1.

**Failure** The creature is Frightened 2.

**Critical Failure** The creature is Frightened 3 and Paralyzed for 1 round.

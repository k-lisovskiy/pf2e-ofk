# Overview

- **Rank**: 5 (focus spell)
- **Cast**: 2
- **Duration**: Varies
- **Traits**: concentrate, darkness, focus, manipulate

## Description

You dance, Striding up to half your Speed. At either the start or end of your Stride, you create an area of magical darkness in a 10-foot burst centered on your location. This area is filled with magical darkness that works the same way as that created by a Darkness spell of the same rank. When you create the darkness, attempt a Performance check to dance, with a standard DC for your level, to determine the duration of the darkness.

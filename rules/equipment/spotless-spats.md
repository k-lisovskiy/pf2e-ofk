## Description

No soldier in a parade or other formal appearance would dare forget their pristine white spats covering their boots. Their presence inspires the wearer to keep the rest of their uniform equally tidy. While wearing the *spotless spats*, your outfit is magically cleaned every 10 minutes, as if by the Prestidigitation spell.

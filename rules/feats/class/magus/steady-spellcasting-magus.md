- **Category**: Class (magus)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: magus

## Description

Confident in your technique, you don't easily lose your concentration when you Cast a Spell.

If a reaction would disrupt your spellcasting action, attempt a flat check (DC 15) check. If you succeed, your action isn't disrupted.

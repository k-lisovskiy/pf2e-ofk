# Overview

- **Level**: 14
- **Category**: Class (shared-class-feats)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Traits**: barbarian, druid, emotion, fear, mental, rage, visual

## Description

**Trigger** Your size increases due to a polymorph effect.

---

The physical growth of your transformation is a spectacle that shakes foes to their core. Each enemy smaller than your new size within 30 feet of you must attempt a will save save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

---

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** The creature becomes Frightened 1.

**Critical Failure** The creature becomes Frightened 2, and you can push it up to 10 feet from you. This is forced movement.

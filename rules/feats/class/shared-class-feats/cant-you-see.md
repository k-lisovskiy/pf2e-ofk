# Overview

- **Level**: 8
- **Category**: Class (shared-class-feats)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: trained in Occultism, expert in Stealth
- **Traits**: druid, ranger

## Description

The eye rebels, the mind recoils—no matter how much those who can see you try to explain what's there, their friends' gazes just skitter over you, like a bird afraid to land. A character who attempts to Point Out your location must attempt a flat check (DC 14). If they fail, their allies misunderstand them and aren't sure where you are. On a critical failure, their allies think they pointed you out in a different location entirely, chosen by the GM. Similarly, when a creature critically fails to [[/act seek]] you while you're Hidden to or Undetected by it, it thinks you're in a different location chosen by the GM. In either case, you appear to be hidden to a creature that thinks you're in a different location, though you're actually undetected by it for targeting and further uses of the Seek action.

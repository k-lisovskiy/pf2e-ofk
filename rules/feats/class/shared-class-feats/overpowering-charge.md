# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Class (shared-class-feats)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Barreling Charge
- **Traits**: barbarian, fighter

## Description

You trample your foes as you charge past them. When you use Barreling Charge and successfully move through a creature's space, that creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier. If you critically succeed, the creature takes double this amount of damage and becomes Off-Guard until the end of your next turn.

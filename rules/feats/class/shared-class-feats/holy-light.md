# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Class (shared-class-feats)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: you follow a good-aligned deity
- **Traits**: champion, cleric, concentrate, divine, light

## Description

You raise your arms to the sky and pray fervently, surrounding yourself in a beacon of holy light. The beacon sheds bright light in a 30-foot cylinder centered on you until the beginning of your next turn. Evil creatures in the area must succeed at a will save save equal to your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) or become Dazzled for 1 round.

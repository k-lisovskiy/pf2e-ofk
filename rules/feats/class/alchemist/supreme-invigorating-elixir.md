# Overview

- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Invigorating Elixir

## Description

For the purposes of counteracting effects with invigorating elixirs, treat your level as though it were 2 higher. Add the listed conditions to those you can choose for an invigorating elixir you create to counteract: Petrified, Stunned. You can also choose a disease regardless of what, if any, conditions it imposes.

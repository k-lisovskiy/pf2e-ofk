# Overview

- **Level**: 20
- **Category**: Class (alchemist)
- **Action**: Free Action
- **Frequency**: 1 per minute
- **Traits**: additive2, alchemist

## Description

**Frequency** once per minute

**Trigger** You use Advanced Alchemy to craft an alchemical bomb that deals splash damage and is at least 2 levels lower than your Advanced Alchemy level.

---

Your bomb incorporates an explosive spark of wonder that leaves witnesses dumbfounded. Any target hit by your Strike with the bomb is Fascinated by you for 1 round. On a critical success, the target is also Stunned 1.

- **Category**: Class (alchemist)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: alchemist

## Description

A combination of additional viscosity and careful application keeps your weapons poisoned even when suffering significant wear and tear.

If your Strike with a poisoned weapon would expend its poison without your target attempting an initial save (due to resistance or your Strike being a critical failure, for example), attempt a flat check (DC 5). On a success, your weapon remains poisoned.

If your Strike with a poisoned weapon succeeds, attempt a flat check (DC 17). On a success, your weapon remains poisoned until the end of your next turn.

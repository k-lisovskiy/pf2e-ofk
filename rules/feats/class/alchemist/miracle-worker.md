## Description

**Frequency** once every 10 minutes

---

Your alchemical mastery can resuscitate the recently slain. You can administer a major or true elixir of life to a creature who has been dead for no more than 2 rounds. When you do, that creature is immediately returned to life with 1 Hit Point and becomes Wounded 1.

# Overview

- **Level**: 1
- **Category**: Class (alchemist)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: alchemist

## Description

You can capably deliver toxins with a Blowgun. Your blowgun Strikes can apply injury poisons even if they deal no damage due to a creature's resistance. If you critically succeed at a blowgun Strike using a poisoned dart, the target's initial save against the poison is one degree of success worse than the creature rolls; this is a misfortune effect.

In addition, if you make a blowgun Strike while Hidden or Undetected, you don't automatically become Observed. Instead, immediately attempt a stealth save check against the Perception DC of the target. If you succeed, you don't become observed, and are hidden (if you were undetected, you still become hidden rather than remaining undetected).

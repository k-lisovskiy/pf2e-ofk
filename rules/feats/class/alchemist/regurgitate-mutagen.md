# Overview

- **Level**: 4
- **Category**: Class (alchemist)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Traits**: alchemist, manipulate

## Description

**Requirements** You are under the effects of a mutagen.

---

You redirect a mutagen within your body to spit a stream of stomach acid at a foe. A creature within 30 feet takes 1d6 acid damage for every 2 levels you have ((floor(actor level/2))d6 acid), with a reflex save save against your class DC. On a failure, the creature is also Sickened 1 (or Sickened 2 on a critical failure). The mutagen's duration immediately ends.

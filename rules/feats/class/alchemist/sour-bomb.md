# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Class (alchemist)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: additive, alchemist, olfactory

## Description

You can mix ingredients with a powerful sour smell into an alchemical bomb to make its explosion an overpowering stench. Any creature hit by the bomb must attempt a fortitude save save against your class DC in addition to the bomb's normal effects.

**Success** The creature is unaffected beyond a strong craving for pickles.

**Failure** The creature is Sickened 1.

**Critical Failure** The creature is Sickened 2, and Fleeing for 1 round from the smell.

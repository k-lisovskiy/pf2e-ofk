# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Class (barbarian)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Traits**: barbarian, incapacitation, rage

## Description

A quick strike to the face or mouth silences your opponent. Make a melee Strike against an enemy. If it hits, the enemy must succeed at a fortitude save save against your class DC.

---

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target is dazed and can barely vocalize. It's Stunned 1 and its speech is raspy and hard to understand. It must succeed at a flat check (DC 11) to use linguistic actions or Cast a Spell, unless the spell has the subtle trait.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is Stunned 3 instead of stunned 1.

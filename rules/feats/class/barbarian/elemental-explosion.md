---

You unleash the energy roiling within you, exploding elemental matter in a 15-foot emanation. Each creature in the area takes 1d8 damage per level you possess, with the same type you chose for elemental rage. Each creature in the area must attempt a reflex save. Elemental Explosion gains the trait of your element.

# Overview

- **Level**: 8
- **Category**: Class (guardian)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: guardian

## Description

Your taunts draw the attention of multiple enemies at once. When you use Taunt, you can choose up to three targets within range, and you can have up to three taunted enemies at a time. Each time you Taunt, you can choose which enemies remain taunted and which the effect ends for. You must remain at or below this limit.

# Overview

- **Level**: 18
- **Category**: Class (guardian)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: guardian

## Description

Your armor protects you from the deadliest hits. Each time you're critically hit while wearing the medium or heavy armor, attempt a flat check (DC 14). On a success, it becomes a normal hit.

---

**Special** If your armor has a Fortification rune, you roll only one flat check, but the DC is 12 (or 10 if it's a Greater Fortification rune).

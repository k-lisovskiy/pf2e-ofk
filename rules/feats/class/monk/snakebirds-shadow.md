- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: Waterfowl Stance

## Description

**Requirements** You are in Waterfowl Stance and are wielding a monk weapon from the sword group.

---

Ganhil developed this technique through observing the sharp-beaked darters fishing Prada Hanam's waters, seeking to match their speed and suppleness with the elemental flash of his blade. Make a melee Strike with the required weapon against each enemy in a 15-foot type:line as razor-sharp shadows of water surge from your blade. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but you do not increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.

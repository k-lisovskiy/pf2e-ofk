# Overview

- **Level**: 2
- **Category**: Class (monk)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: monk, water

## Description

Following your studies of water, your qi stirs the water of the natural world. If you're underwater, you have cover against creatures outside of the water. As soon as you meet the prerequisites for Underwater Marauder and Water Sprint, you gain those feats.

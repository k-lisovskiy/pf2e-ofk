## Description

You learn a mystical stance that transforms your qi into sticky smoke that shrouds your limbs. You gain the Clinging Shadows Stance qi spell. While entering the stance is a qi spell, the shadow grasp attacks it grants are not, so you can use them as often as you like while in the stance.

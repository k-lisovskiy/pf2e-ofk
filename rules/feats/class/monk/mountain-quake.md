# Overview

- **Level**: 14
- **Category**: Class (monk)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Prerequisites**: Mountain Stronghold
- **Traits**: monk

## Description

**Requirements** You are in Mountain Stance

---

You stomp, shaking the earth beneath you. Creatures on the ground within a 20-foot emanation take damage equal to your Strength modifier (minimum 0), with a fortitude save save against your class DC. On a failure, they also fall Prone. After you use this action, you can't use it again for [[/r 1d4 #Recharge it]]{1d4 rounds}.

---

**Special** If you have this feat, the Dexterity modifier cap to your AC while using Mountain Stance increases from +1 to +2.

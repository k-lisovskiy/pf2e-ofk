# Overview

- **Level**: 20
- **Category**: Class (cleric)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Prerequisites**: healing font
- **Traits**: cleric

## Description

**Requirements** You have a Heal spell prepared and you aren't Off-Guard to the source of the damage.

**Trigger** Any ally within 30 feet that you can heal with your *heal* spell would take damage from an attack or effect from a source you can observe.

---

You can manipulate the same energies that the *aeon orbs* use to sustain life. You cast *heal* on the triggering target. Instead of its normal effects, the *heal* spell reduces the triggering damage by an amount equal to the Hit Points a 1-action *heal* spell of that rank would have recovered. As usual, if this reduces the damage to 0, it might also remove additional effects of the triggering attack, such as injury poisons.

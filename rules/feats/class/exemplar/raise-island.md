# Overview

- **Level**: 8
- **Category**: Class (exemplar)
- **Action**: 3 actions
- **Frequency**: 1 per hour
- **Prerequisites**: born of the bones of the earth or restless as the tide
- **Traits**: concentrate, divine, exemplar

## Description

**Frequency** once per hour

---

You churn the sea and fish up stone so your enemies together can receive their punishment. Each enemy in a 30-foot type:emanation must succeed at a fortitude save save against your class DC or be swept up to 15 feet to another location of your choice within the affected area and become Off-Guard until the start of your next turn. Then, if you're on a surface, a pillar of earth rises underneath you and lasts for 1 minute. The pillar is 10 feet tall, and its surface includes your space and a 5-foot type:emanation around you. After the pillar rises, you can attempt a melee Strike against one enemy in reach.

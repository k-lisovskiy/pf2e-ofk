## Description

**Requirements** You critically hit an enemy this turn.

---

You let out a war cry as your blow lands true, and judgment descends on an enemy. A pillar of light deals ternary(gte(actor level,17),10,ternary(gte(actor level,12),7,4))d6 spirit damage with a reflex save save against your class DC to a different enemy selected at random within 30 feet of the target struck. This damage increases to 7d6 if you are 12th level and 10d6 if you are 17th level.

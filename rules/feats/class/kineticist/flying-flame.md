# Overview

- **Level**: 1
- **Category**: Class (kineticist)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Traits**: fire, impulse, kineticist, primal

## Description

A Tiny shape of flame appears, taking a form of your choice—such as a bird, an arrow, or a simple sphere. It Flies from you up to 30 feet in a path you choose. Each creature it passes through takes (floor((actor level -1)/2)+1)d6 fire damage with a reflex save save against your class DC. A creature attempts only one save, even if the flame passes through it multiple times.

---

**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.

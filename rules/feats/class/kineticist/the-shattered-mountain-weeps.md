## Description

A massive sphere of rock explodes, unleashing a cataclysm of falling debris and deadly shrapnel. Its destruction falls in a 20-foot burst within 120 feet. Creatures in the area take (ternary(gte(actor level,20),10,9))d10 bludgeoning]|options:area-damage damage with a fortitude save save against your class DC. Those who fail are knocked Prone. For the next minute, rocks continue to fall, making the area difficult terrain and dealing (ternary(gte(actor level,20),4,3))d10 bludgeoning]|options:area-damage damage to any creature that ends its turn in the area. If you use this impulse again, any previous one ends.

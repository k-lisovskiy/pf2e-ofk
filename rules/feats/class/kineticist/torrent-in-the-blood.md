# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Class (kineticist)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Traits**: healing, impulse, kineticist, manipulate, overflow, primal, vitality, water

## Description

A healing wave splashes across creatures in a 30-foot cone, its cleansing water driving afflictions from the body. Each creature in the area regains (floor((max(6,actor level)-6)/2)+3)d8 healing HP and can attempt a new save against one poison or disease affliction affecting it; on a failed save, the condition doesn't worsen.

Each creature that benefited from this impulse becomes temporarily immune to Torrent in the Blood for 10 minutes.

---

**Level (+2)** The healing increases by 1d8.

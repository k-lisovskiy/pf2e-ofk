## Description

Gleaming flakes of chilling snow fall in a 10-foot burst within 60 feet. Each creature in the area takes (floor((actor level -1)/2)+2)d4 cold]|options:area-damage damage with a reflex save save against your class DC. The ground in the area is covered in a snow drift, which is difficult terrain. Each square of the drift lasts until it melts, either naturally or until fire damage is dealt in that square.

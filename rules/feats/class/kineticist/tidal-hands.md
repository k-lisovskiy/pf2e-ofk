## Description

With an emphatic gesture, you create waves that rush out from you in the shape of your hands. You either form two 15-foot cone that don't overlap or you combine the hands into one 30-foot cone. Each creature in a wave takes (floor((actor level -1)/2)+1)d8 bludgeoning]|options:area-damage damage with a reflex save save against your class DC. If a creature critically fails, you also push that creature 5 feet.

---

**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.

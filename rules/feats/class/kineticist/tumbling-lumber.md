# Overview

- **Level**: 4
- **Category**: Class (kineticist)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Traits**: impulse, kineticist, primal, wood

## Description

A slew of logs eject from the Plane of Wood and slam into your enemies. They roll in a 30-foot line. Non-magical difficult terrain on any ground the logs roll over is smashed flat; greater difficult terrain remains. Each creature in the area takes (floor((actor level -4)/3)+2)d8 bludgeoning]|options:area-damage damage and might be moved by the logs, depending on its reflex save save against your class DC.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is pushed out of the line in the most direct path available.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is knocked Prone.

---

**Level (+3)** The damage increases by 1d8.

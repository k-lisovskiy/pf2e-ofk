# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Class (kineticist)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Traits**: fire, impulse, kineticist, overflow, primal

## Description

**Trigger** An enemy in your kinetic aura damages you.

---

Lava reflexively explodes underneath you and the creature that hurt you. The triggering enemy takes (floor((actor level -6)/4)+1)d6 fire damage with a reflex save save against your class DC. You Leap up to half your Speed in any direction; this movement doesn't trigger reactions.

---

**Level (+4)** The damage increases by 1d6.

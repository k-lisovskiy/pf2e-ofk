## Description

Jagged metal shards form in the air and lash out from you. You choose shards or spines, which changes the area, damage type, and critical failure effect. Each creature in the area attempts a reflex save save against your class DC.

**Shards** Shards deal (floor((actor level -1)/2)+1)d6 slashing]|options:area-damage damage in a 15-foot cone, and a creature that critically fails takes 1d6 bleed damage.

**Spines** Spines deal (floor((actor level -1)/2)+1)d6 piercing]|options:area-damage damage in a 30-foot line, and a creature that critically fails is Clumsy 1 until the start of your next turn.

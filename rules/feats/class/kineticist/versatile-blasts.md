## Description

Accessing a wider realm of elemental manifestations, you can make blasts with rare forms of your elements. Add the following damage types to those you can choose for Elemental Blasts of that element:

- **air** cold

- **earth** poison

- **fire** cold

- **metal** electricity

- **water** acid

- **wood** poison

# Overview

- **Level**: 8
- **Category**: Class (inventor)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Megaton Strike
- **Traits**: inventor

## Description

When you use a full-power Megaton Strike, you can knock your foe back. When you succeed at your Strike while using an unstable Megaton Strike, your target must attempt a fortitude save save against your class DC.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is pushed back 5 feet.

**Failure** The creature is pushed back 10 feet.

**Critical Failure** The creature is pushed back 20 feet.

---

**Special** If your innovation is a minion, this benefit applies on its unstable Megaton Strikes.

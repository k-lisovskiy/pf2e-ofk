# Overview

- **Level**: 2
- **Category**: Class (inventor)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Traits**: fire, healing, inventor, manipulate, unstable

## Description

They told you there was no way that explosions could heal people, but they were fools… Fools who didn't understand your brilliance! You create a minor explosion from your innovation, altering the combustion to cauterize wounds using vaporized medicinal herbs. You or a living creature adjacent to you regains max(1,ceil(actor level/2))d10 healing Hit Points. In addition, the creature you heal can attempt an immediate flat check to recover from a single source of persistent bleed damage, with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.

At 3rd level, and every 2 levels thereafter, increase the healing by 1d10.

---

**Special** If your innovation is a minion, it can take this action rather than you, though because it's not a living creature, it can't use the ability on itself.

# Overview

- **Level**: 2
- **Category**: Class (inventor)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Prerequisites**: armor innovation
- **Traits**: fire, inventor, manipulate, unstable

## Description

**Requirements** You have a foe Grabbed.

---

Your armor includes flame-resistant gauntlets with oil-filled finger joints. These joints can split apart, dousing your opponent in flammable oil and then igniting it. The opponent must attempt a reflex save save against your class DC.

---

**Critical Success** The grab ends.

**Success** The grab ends, and the target takes 1 persistent fire damage.

**Failure** The target takes (floor(actor level/2)) persistent fire damage equal to half your level.

**Critical Failure** The target takes (actor level) persistent fire damage equal to your level.

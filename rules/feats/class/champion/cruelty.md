## Description

The harm you deal with your corrupting touch saps the strength from your enemy's body. You can cast Touch of the Void targeting a living creature using 2 actions instead of 1. If you do, the target is also Enfeebled 1 for 1 minute if it fails its save (Enfeebled 2 if it critically fails).

- **Level**: 8
- **Category**: Class (champion)

- **Prerequisites**: Faithful Steed, lay on hands

## Description

Your devotion to your mount manifests as a surge of vital energy.

When you cast Lay on Hands on your mount, instead of the normal amount, the spell restores 10 Hit Points, plus 10 for each heightened rank.

# Overview

- **Level**: 12
- **Category**: Class (oracle)
- **Action**: Free Action
- **Frequency**: 1 per day
- **Traits**: divine, oracle

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** Your turn begins.

**Requirements** You are Unconscious and have the Dying condition.

---

The crossroads between life and death is a place that can reveal many secrets. You gain the effects of an Augury spell in a strange, near-death vision. Empowered by the realization, you then lose the dying condition (becoming Wounded 1 or increasing your wounded value by 1, as normal), regain 2*actor level healing Hit Points, and can Stand.

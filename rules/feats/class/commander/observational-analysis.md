## Description

You are able to rapidly discern relevant details about your opponents in the heat of combat. When you use Combat Assessment against a target that you or an ally has targeted with a Strike or spell since the start of your last turn, you get a +2 circumstance bonus to the Recall Knowledge check (+4 if the Strike from Combat Assessment is a critical hit.)

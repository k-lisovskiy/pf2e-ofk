- **Prerequisites**: master in Athletics

## Description

**Trigger** A Prone foe within your reach Stands.

---

You have ways of keeping your foes down. Attempt an athletics save check against the triggering foe's Fortitude DC. On a success, the action is disrupted, causing the creature to remain prone. On a critical success, the creature also can't Stand until its next turn.

# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Class (rogue)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: rogue

## Description

You can Disarm a creature through sleight of hand rather than brute force. You can use [[/act disarm skill=thievery]]{Thievery} instead of Athletics when you attempt a Disarm. When you use Thievery to attempt a Disarm and succeed, the target is Off-Guard against the next attack you make before the end of your turn.

# Overview

- **Level**: 2
- **Category**: Class (investigator)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: investigator

## Description

The plans you make include your allies as well as yourself. When you hit a creature with an attack on which you substituted your attack roll due to Devising a Stratagem, designate one ally. The creature you hit is Off-Guard to that ally on the next attack that ally makes against that creature before the start of your next turn.

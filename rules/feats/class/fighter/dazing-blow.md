# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Class (fighter)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Traits**: fighter, press

## Description

**Requirements** You have a creature Grabbed.

---

You pummel a held foe, hoping to stagger them. Make a melee Strike against a creature you have grabbed. The weapon damage from this Strike is bludgeoning damage. If the Strike hits, the creature must attempt a fortitude save save against your class DC; this is an incapacitation effect.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is Stunned 1.

**Failure** The creature is Stunned 2.

**Critical Failure** The creature is Stunned 3.

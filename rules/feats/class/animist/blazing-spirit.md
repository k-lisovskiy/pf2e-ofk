# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Class (animist)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Frequency**: 1 per 10 minutes
- **Traits**: animist, apparition, divine, fire, wandering

## Description

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

**Trigger** A creature damages you with a melee attack.

**Requirements** Your attuned apparition grants Battlegrounds Lore or Volcano Lore as an apparition skill.

---

Your apparition grants fiery defenses. You gain resistance equal to your level against the triggering damage, and the triggering creature takes (ternary(gte(actor level,18),3,ternary(gte(actor level,12),2,1)))d6[fire],(ternary(gte(actor level,18),3,ternary(gte(actor level,12),2,1))) persistent fire damage. This damage increases to 2d6 fire damage and 2 persistent fire damage at 12th level, and 3d6 fire damage and 3 persistent fire damage at 18th level.

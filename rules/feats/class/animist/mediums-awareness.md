# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Class (animist)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: animist, apparition, divine, wandering

## Description

Your apparitions watch over you. You gain a +2 status bonus to Perception checks made to Seek and when using Perception for your initiative roll. At 12th level, this status bonus increases to +3, and at 20th level, to +4.

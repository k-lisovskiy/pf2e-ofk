## Description

You've learned to communicate with creatures from all over the world, overcoming language barriers to lend a hand and help where you're needed. You're affected by a constant Truespeech spell.

When you attempt a Diplomacy check to Make an Impression or Request, you can spend 1 Mythic Point to make the check at mythic proficiency.

- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: Apocalypse Rider Dedication
- **Traits**: concentrate, mythic

## Description

You spoil the food and water nearby, even within your foes' bodies. Spend a Mythic Point. Enemies within 60 feet of you must succeed at a fortitude save save against your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) or become Sickened 1. You also cause edible food to rot and potable water to turn brackish within the area. Choose a number of potions or alchemical elixirs within the area equal to your key attribute modifier and attempt a counteract against each of them using occultism save or religion save at mythic proficiency. Your counteract rank is half your level. If you succeed, the elixir or potion spoils and becomes a mundane item.

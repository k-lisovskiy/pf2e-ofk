# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Archetype (werecreature)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: Werecreature Dedication, your animal shape grants a claw unarmed attack
- **Traits**: archetype

## Description

**Requirements** You're in hybrid or animal shape.

---

Sinking your claws in, you propel yourself at shocking speed. You Climb up to your land Speed and make a claw Strike at any point during that movement. After you finish the movement, attempt an athletics save check against the surface's Climb DC.

---

**Success** You maintain your position.

**Failure** You slide down 60 feet or to the ground without taking falling damage.

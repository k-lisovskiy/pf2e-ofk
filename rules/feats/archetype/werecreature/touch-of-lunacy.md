# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Archetype (werecreature)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Werecreature Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, curse, morph, primal

## Description

In moments of viciousness, your curse can partially infect your prey. Whenever you critically succeed with an unarmed attack from the werecreature archetype, the target must attempt a fortitude save save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC, or become Clumsy 2 until the beginning of your next turn as their bones elongate and form warps. On a critical failure, the target is also Clumsy 1 for one minute as the transformation lingers.

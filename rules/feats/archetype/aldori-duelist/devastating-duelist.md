## Description

You pointedly note your enemy's deficiencies, filling them with fear and dismay. Strike an opponent with your Aldori Dueling Sword.

If the Strike is successful, attempt a dueling-lore save check against the target's Will DC. On a success, the target becomes Frightened 2. This is an emotion, fear, and mental effect.

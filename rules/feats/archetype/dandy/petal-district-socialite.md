- **Prerequisites**: Dandy Dedication

## Description

Through your experience with the many parties held nightly in clubs and estates within the Petal District, you understand the way rumors and gossip circulate during social events. You can use the Influence Rumor activity during a social event attended by a large group of people, such as a gala or town fair, reducing the time it takes to [[/gmr 1d4 #hours]]{1d4 hours}. This ability doesn't apply to secret events or other small private gatherings.

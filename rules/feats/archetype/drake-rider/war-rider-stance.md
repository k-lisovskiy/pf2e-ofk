## Description

Your dragon's constant breath threatens your foes. While in this stance, your dragon companion has an aura in a 5-foot type:emanation. Each creature that starts its turn in the aura takes the damage listed under the dragon's support benefit with a basic Reflex save against your mount's Athletics DC.

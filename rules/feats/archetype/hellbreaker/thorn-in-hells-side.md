## Description

Your devotion to the ideals of justice, freedom, and revolution allow you to truly punish those who stand in your way. When you critically succeed at a Hellbreaker Strike, the target must succeed at a fortitude save save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or become Stunned 1 (Stunned 2 on a critical failure). This effect has the incapacitation trait.

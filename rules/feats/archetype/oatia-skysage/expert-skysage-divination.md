## Description

Your studies of the stars have taught you about the mysteries of the cosmos. You choose one of the following 4th-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: Clairvoyance or Read Omens. At 12th level, choose one of the following 5th-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: Mind Probe or Scouting Eye. At 14th level, choose one of the following 6th-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: Scrying or Truesight. You become an expert with spell attack rolls and spell DCs for occult spells.

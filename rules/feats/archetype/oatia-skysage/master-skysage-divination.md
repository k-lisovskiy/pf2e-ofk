## Description

You unlock the most powerful secrets hidden in the stars above. You choose one of the following 7th-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: Retrocognition or True Target. At 18th level, add Pinpoint to your spell repertoire, and at 20th level, add Foresight to your spell repertoire. You gain master proficiency with spell attack rolls and spell DCs for occult spells.

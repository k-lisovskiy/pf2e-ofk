# Overview

- **Level**: 8
- **Category**: Archetype (pure-legion-enforcer)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: Pure Legion Enforcer Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, auditory, linguistic

## Description

You recite an excerpt of the Laws of Mortality, often the first law: "Let no mortal be beholden to a god." Attempt an intimidation save check against all enemies within 60 feet that worship a deity, comparing your result against each target's Will DC. Targets are then temporarily immune to your use of this ability for 24 hours.

---

**Critical Success** The target becomes Frightened 2. Until the start of your next turn, if the target attempts to Cast a Spell with the divine trait, it must succeed at a flat check (DC 7) or the action is lost.

**Success** As critical success, but Frightened 1, and the DC of the flat check is 5.

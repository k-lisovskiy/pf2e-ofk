# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Archetype (palatine-detective)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Frequency**: 1 per day
- **Prerequisites**: Palatine Detective Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, concentrate, healing, manipulate

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

---

Using various implements and esoteric methods, you can attempt to remove a curse from an unfortunate victim. It takes 10 minutes to perform these rites on a willing creature, who must remain within your reach the entire time.

Attempt to counteract one curse afflicting the creature; your counteract rank is half your level rounded up, and you use either your occultism save or religion save modifier as your counteract modifier. If the afflicted creature or the individual who placed the curse on the afflicted creature is the target of your active investigation through Pursue a Lead, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to this counteract check.

If the curse comes from a cursed item or other external source, a success indicates that the target creature can rid itself of the cursed item, but it doesn't remove the curse from the item.

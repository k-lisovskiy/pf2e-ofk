# Overview

- **Level**: 4
- **Category**: Archetype (palatine-detective)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Palatine Detective Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, skill

## Description

Thanks to your constant involvement with cryptic texts, you have attained an almost-supernatural ability to decipher complex codes and understand ancient writings. You can always use [[/act decipher-writing skill=occultism]]{Occultism} or [[/act decipher-writing skill=religion]]{Religion} when you Decipher Writing, no matter the subject matter. In addition, you can ignore language constraints when Deciphering Writing, and when you critically fail to Decipher Writing, you get a failure instead.

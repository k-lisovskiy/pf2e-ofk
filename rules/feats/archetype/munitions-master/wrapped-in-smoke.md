## Description

You ignite a small portion of powder in a rapid, inefficient burn, creating a hazy cloud. You create a cloud of smoke in a 5-foot type:emanation centered on your innovation, which lasts for 1 minute. All creatures and objects within the smoke become Concealed, and those outside the smoke become concealed to those within.

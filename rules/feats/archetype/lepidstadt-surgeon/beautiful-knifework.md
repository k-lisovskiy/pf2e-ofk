# Overview

- **Level**: 7
- **Category**: Archetype (lepidstadt-surgeon)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Lepidstadt Surgeon Dedication, master in Medicine
- **Traits**: archetype, exploration, manipulate, skill

## Description

You perform surgical adjustments to disguise a willing ally or your construct companion. This counts as preparing a disguise to Impersonate, taking 10 minutes and requiring a healer's toolkit instead of a disguise kit. The DC to see through the deception with Seek is your Medicine DC (perception save) unless the disguised character's Deception DC is higher. If the target directly interacts with someone while disguised, they gain a +2 circumstance bonus to their Deception check.

You can reverse this effect with another 10-minute surgery. The disguised character can also end it as a free action when they receive any healing effect.

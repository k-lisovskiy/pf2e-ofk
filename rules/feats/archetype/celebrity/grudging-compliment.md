# Overview

- **Level**: 7
- **Category**: Archetype (celebrity)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Prerequisites**: Celebrity Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, linguistic, skill

## Description

**Trigger** An enemy within 30 feet damages you or succeeds at a skill check.

---

Every Firebrand can recognize the skill of others, but you've turned it into an art you can wield in combat. You compliment a foe's form or technique, and your unexpected praise distracts them from their surroundings. Attempt a diplomacy save check against that enemy's Will DC. Regardless of your result, they are temporarily immune to your Grudging Compliments for 10 minutes.

---

**Critical Success** The target becomes Off-Guard for 1 round.

**Success** The target becomes off-guard against your attacks for 1 round.

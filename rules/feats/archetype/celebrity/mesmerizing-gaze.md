# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Archetype (celebrity)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: Celebrity Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, emotion, mental, visual

## Description

When you meet someone's gaze, they're unable to look away from you. Choose one target creature you can see and that can see you. That creature must succeed at a will save save or be Fascinated with you until the end of your next turn; the DC is the higher of your class DC or spell DC. If the creature succeeds at its save or its fascination ends due to a hostile action, it becomes temporarily immune to your Mesmerizing Gaze for 1 day.

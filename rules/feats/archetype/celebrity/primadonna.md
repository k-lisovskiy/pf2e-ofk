# Overview

- **Level**: 12
- **Category**: Archetype (celebrity)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Frequency**: 1 per hour
- **Prerequisites**: Celebrity Dedication, master in Performance
- **Traits**: archetype, auditory, visual

## Description

**Frequency** once per hour

---

The spotlight should always be on you and you alone. Attempt a performance save check against the Will DCs of all enemies within 30-foot emanation who can see or hear you. On a success, the targets focus on you and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to hit you, but they're Off-Guard to all creatures other than yourself. On a critical success, the effect lasts for 3 rounds.

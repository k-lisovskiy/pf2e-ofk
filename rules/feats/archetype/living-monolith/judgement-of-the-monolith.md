# Overview

- **Level**: 12
- **Category**: Archetype (living-monolith)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Ka Stone Ritual
- **Traits**: archetype

## Description

You can use the oaths bound in your ka stone to compel the truth from the living and the dead. You can activate your ka stone to target a single living creature, causing that creature to experience the effects of being in a Ring of Truth. You can also activate it with a 10-minute activation (envision) to cast Talking Corpse.

# Overview

- **Level**: 12
- **Category**: Archetype (spell-trickster)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Spell Trickster Dedication, ability to cast cloudkill
- **Traits**: archetype

## Description

When you cast Toxic Cloud, modify its duration to be sustained up to 1 minute, and modify its standard effect to include the following: The cloud doesn't move away from you each round. Once per round when you Sustain the Spell, you can move the cloud 10 feet in the direction of your choice.

## Description

**Access** You're an aristocrat or honored retainer from a Chelaxian noble house.

---

A noble house has invested in your training, imparting special techniques the family has honed over generations. You gain Additional Lore as a skill feat, choosing from among Cheliax Lore, Devil Lore, Hell Lore, or a Lore associated with your house. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception checks, Diplomacy checks, Intimidation checks, and your Will DC against Chelaxian citizens and devils.

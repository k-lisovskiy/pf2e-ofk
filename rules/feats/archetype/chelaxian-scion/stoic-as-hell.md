# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Archetype (chelaxian-scion)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Frequency**: 1 per minute
- **Prerequisites**: Chelaxian Scion Dedication, trained in Intimidation, expert in Will
- **Traits**: archetype

## Description

**Trigger** You're targeted by an emotion effect or effect that would give you the Controlled condition.

---

Like the archdevil Dispater, you maintain an unshakable poise even under impossible stress. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the triggering saving throw or to your Will DC against the triggering effect. If you critically succeed at the saving throw (or if the source of the effect critically fails its skill check, such as to Demoralize you), you gain temporary Hit Points equal to half your level until the end of the effect's source's next turn. If the effect originated from a creature, you can [[/act demoralize]] that creature once as a free action on your next turn; if the creature was temporarily immune to your attempts to Demoralize it, it loses that immunity.

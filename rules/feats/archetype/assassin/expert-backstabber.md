## Description

You excel at quickly dispatching your foes. When you Strike an Off-Guard foe with a weapon that has the backstabber weapon trait, you deal 2 extra precision damage instead of 1. If the weapon is a *+3 weapon*, you deal 4 extra precision damage instead of 2.

## Description

**Trigger** You or an ally within 60 feet attempts a saving throw against a fear effect.

---

With a simple reminder of perseverance, you bolster the bravery of yourself and your allies. You attempt to counteract the triggering effect with a warfare-lore save check, using half your level rounded up as the counteract rank.

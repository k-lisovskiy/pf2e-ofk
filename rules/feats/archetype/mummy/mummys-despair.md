# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Archetype (mummy)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Frequency**: 1 per hour
- **Prerequisites**: Mummy Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, aura, divine, emotion, fear, mental

## Description

**Frequency** once per hour

---

You force your mental anguish outward, projecting it upon those around you. You gain an aura of despair in a 30-foot emanation lasting 5 rounds. A creature that enters or begins its turn in the aura must succeed at a will save save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC or be Frightened 1 (Frightened 2 on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at the save is temporarily immune to Mummy's Despair for 10 minutes.

# Overview

- **Level**: 4
- **Category**: Archetype (gladiator)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Prerequisites**: Gladiator Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, concentrate

## Description

**Trigger** You reduce an enemy to 0 Hit Points during a non-trivial combat encounter with spectators.

---

You show off for the crowd. Attempt a performance save check; the DC is determined by the GM but is typically the standard DC for your level or the DC to Make an Impression on the spectators, whichever is higher. On a success, choose one of the following benefits; on a critical success, choose two benefits:

- A number of temporary Hit Points equal to your character level; these last for 1 minute.

Effect: Play to the Crowd (Temporary Hit Points)

- A +1 circumstance bonus to AC until the end of your next turn.

Effect: Play to the Crowd (AC Bonus)

- A +1 circumstance bonus to your next attack roll before the end of your next turn.

Effect: Play to the Crowd (Attack Roll Bonus)

## Description

You learn how to create a luminous reservoir of vitality energy that you can use to punish undead who dare attack you. You gain the domain spell Vital Luminance. It costs 1 Focus Point to cast a focus spell. This feat grants a focus pool of 1 Focus Point that you can recover using the Refocus activity.

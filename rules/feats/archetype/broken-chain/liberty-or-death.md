- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: Broken Chain Dedication

## Description

**Requirements** A creature has been the target of your Ultimatum of Liberation for at least 1 minute

---

You have given a despot enough time to think, and now is the time for them to forfeit their control or die. Spend a Mythic Point and make a Strike at mythic proficiency against the required creature. If you succeed and deal damage, the target must succeed on a fortitude save save against your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) or die. If the target succeeds, they are immune to this ability for 24 hours.

## Description

**Requirements** You're wielding a fan.

---

Your fans, one raised up alongside your head and the other alongside your hip, become a blur as you twirl across the battlefield. Attempt to Tumble Through an enemy's space using [[/act tumble-through skill=performance]]{Performance}; on a success, you can make a melee Strike against the enemy with a fan you're wielding at any point during the movement. On a critical success, the enemy is Off-Guard against this attack.

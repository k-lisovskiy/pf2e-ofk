## Description

You gain a tattoo that serves as a special ward to defend you against Walkena's forces.

The tattoo allows you to cast the Light cantrip as a divine innate spell at will. The cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. If you die, the tattoo immediately casts Peaceful Rest on your corpse, heightened to the same spell rank.

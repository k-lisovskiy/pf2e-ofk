## Description

Ishii Bunji is a skilled tumbler, and learning his technique allows you to push yourself off a foe and propel yourself across the battlefield faster. If you succeed at an Acrobatics check to Tumble Through an enemy's space, you can Stride again as a free action after you complete your current movement.

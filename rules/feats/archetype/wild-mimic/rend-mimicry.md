- **Level**: 4
- **Category**: Archetype (wild-mimic)

## Description

**Requirements** You have hit the same enemy with two consecutive melee Strikes that each dealt slashing damage.

---

Taking advantage of the brutal efficiency of hacking away at a target with two slashing attacks, you've mastered the art of punishing a foe caught between your slices. You deal (ternary(gte(actor level,16),3,ternary(gte(actor level,10),2,1)))d6 slashing damage to the target. This damage increases to 2d6 at 10th level and to 3d6 at 16th level.

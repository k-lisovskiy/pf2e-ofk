# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Archetype (wild-mimic)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Frequency**: 1 per 10 minutes
- **Prerequisites**: Wild Mimic Dedication, you have seen a creature deal electricity damage with an unarmed Strike or have identified a creature capable of dealing electricity damage with an unarmed Strike in combat
- **Traits**: archetype, electricity, primal

## Description

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

---

Some animals and beasts have electric organs in their body that they can use to jolt their prey. You may not have the requisite physiology to do the same, but a touch of primal magic can achieve the same effect. Make a melee unarmed Strike against a creature. If the Strike hits, it deals an additional 1d12 electricity damage, and the creature must make a fortitude save save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is Clumsy 1 for 1 round.

**Failure** The creature is Clumsy 2 for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The creature is Clumsy 3 for 1 round.

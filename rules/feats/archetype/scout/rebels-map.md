---

Rebellions require an intricate knowledge of one's surroundings—because you might need to escape at any moment. You remember the locations of escape routes, ambush spots, and cover from a scouting expedition or a previous visit to the area. Roll a [[/r 1d20]]{d20}. At any point during the subsequent combat, you can substitute the result of this roll instead of rolling a d20 on one Deception check to Create a Diversion or on one Stealth check to Hide.

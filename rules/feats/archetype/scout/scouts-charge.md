## Description

You meander around unpredictably, and then ambush your opponents without warning.

Choose one enemy. Stride, Feint against that opponent, and then make a Strike against it. For your Feint, you can attempt a [[/act feint skill=stealth]]{Stealth} check instead of the Deception check that's usually required, using the terrain around you to surprise your foe.

## Description

Your church ensures that you are cared for. You can use Religion to [[/act coerce skill=religion]]{Coerce}, [[/act gather-information skill=religion]]{Gather Information}, [[/act make-an-impression skill=religion]]{Make an Impression}, or [[/act request skill=religion]]{Request} as long as you are in a town or city with a church dedicated to your deity. If the target of the check is also a worshipper of your deity or is your hunted prey, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the check.

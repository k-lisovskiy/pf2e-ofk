# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Archetype (worm-caller)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Frequency**: 1 per day
- **Prerequisites**: Worm Caller Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, primal

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

---

You call forth a brood of infantile cave worms to feast on your enemy's flesh. Choose a creature within 30 feet; the creature must attempt a fortitude save save. At 8th level and every 2 levels above that, the persistent piercing damage taken on a failure increases by 1d6.

---

**Critical Success** The worms writhe away harmlessly.

**Success** The target takes 2d6[piercing],2d6 persistent piercing damage.

**Failure** The target takes 6d6[piercing],(max(2,floor((actor level -2)/2)))d6 persistent piercing damage.

**Critical Failure** As failure, plus the target is Off-Guard as long as it takes persistent piercing damage.

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

---

You call forth a ghostly manifestation of the worm spirit. Choose an intersection on the ground within 60 feet; the ghostly maw of a worm emerges from the ground, attacking all creatures in a 10-foot burst. Each creature in the area must attempt a reflex save save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes (1d10+13) bludgeoning]|options:area-damage damage as it is battered by the worm's body.

**Failure** The target takes (3d10+15) piercing]|options:area-damage damage as it is bitten by the worm. The target is Immobilized until the end of its next turn.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the damage increases to (6d10+30) bludgeoning]|options:area-damage and the target is knocked Prone.

# Overview

- **Level**: 16
- **Category**: Archetype (steel-falcon)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Frequency**: 1 per day
- **Prerequisites**: Steel Falcon Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, aura, divine, emotion, fear, mental

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

---

Your anger at the world's many injustices boil out from you in a wave of palpable energy. An enemy that ends its turn within a 15-foot type:emanation around you must attempt a will save save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. This aura lasts for 1 minute.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune to your Frightening Indignation for 1 minute.

**Success** The creature is Frightened 1.

**Failure** The creature is Frightened 2 and can't reduce its frightened condition below 1 while it's in your aura.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but Frightened 3.

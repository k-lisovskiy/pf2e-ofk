# Overview

- **Level**: 14
- **Category**: Archetype (steel-falcon)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: Steel Falcon Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, auditory, emotion, linguistic, mental

## Description

You weave a confusing web of half-truths, conspiracy theories, and plausible motives to goad an enemy into lashing out wildly. Choose a target within 30 feet that can hear you, and attempt a diplomacy save check against its Will DC. The creature is then immune to Perplex for 24 hours.

---

**Critical Success** The target is Stupefied 3 and Confused for 1 round.

**Success** The target is Stupefied 1 for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is unaffected.

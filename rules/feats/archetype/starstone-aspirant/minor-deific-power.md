## Description

Your mental, physical, and spiritual training have granted you access to divine magic that doesn't rely on any other deity or patron. Choose a common divine cantrip and one common 1st-rank divine spell. You can cast the cantrip as a divine innate spell at will and the 1st-rank spell as a divine innate spell once per day.

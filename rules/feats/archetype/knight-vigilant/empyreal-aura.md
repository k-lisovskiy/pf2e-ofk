- **Prerequisites**: Knight Vigilant Dedication, legendary in Religion
- **Traits**: archetype, aura

## Description

As an ultimate foe of evil and protector of the innocent, your very presence is a ward against evil. Each evil creature within 30-foot emanation of you at the end of your turn must succeed at a will save save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC or else become Slowed 1 (Slowed 2 on a critical failure). On a successful save, the creature is temporarily immune for 10 minutes.

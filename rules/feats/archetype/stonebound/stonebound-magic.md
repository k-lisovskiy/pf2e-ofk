## Description

You've learned to manifest your connection to stone by conjuring it to your side. You gain Scatter Scree as a primal innate cantrip. At 8th level, you gain One with Stone as a primal innate spell once per day. At 10th level, you gain Shape Stone as a primal innate spell once per day. At 12th level, you gain Wall of Stone as a primal innate spell once per day.

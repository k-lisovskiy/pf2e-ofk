## Description

**Requirements** You currently have a fly Speed and are airborne.

---

You hurl away enemies with the force of your landing. You Fly and, if you end your movement on a solid surface, your landing sends out a shock wave of air. Each creature within 10 feet of your landing position must succeed at a reflex save save against your class DC or be pushed 10 feet away. This is forced movement.

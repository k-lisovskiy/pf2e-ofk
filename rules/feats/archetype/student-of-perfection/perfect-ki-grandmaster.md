## Description

You gain an appropriate qi spell for your School of Perfection: Unblinking Flame Ignition for Unblinking Flame, Unbreaking Wave Containment for Unbreaking Waves, Unfolding Wind Crash for Unfolding Wind, or Untwisting Iron Pillar for Untwisting Iron. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. You become a master in occult spell DCs and occult spell attack rolls.

## Description

You gain the appropriate qi spell for your School of Perfection: Unblinking Flame Revelation for Unblinking Flame, Unbreaking Wave Advance for Unbreaking Waves, Unfolding Wind Rush for Unfolding Wind, or Untwisting Iron Buffer for Untwisting Iron.

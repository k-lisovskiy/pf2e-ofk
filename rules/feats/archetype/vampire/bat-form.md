# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Archetype (vampire)

## Description

**Frequency** once per hour

---

You can transform into a bat. You gain the effects of a 4th-rank Pest Form spell. At 14th level, you can choose to spend two actions instead of one to gain the effects of a 4th- or 5th-rank Aerial Form spell instead (bat form only).

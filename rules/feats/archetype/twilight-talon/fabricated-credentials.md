# Overview

- **Level**: 7
- **Category**: Archetype (twilight-talon)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Twilight Talon Dedication, master in Deception
- **Traits**: archetype, skill

## Description

You're so familiar with the paperwork necessary to maintain your cover that you can easily whip up documents that support your fabrications. You can Create a Forgery using [[/act create-forgery skill=deception]]{Deception}, rather than Society, and doing so only takes 10 minutes, or 1 minute if you're legendary in Deception. With your paranoid nature, you're always on the lookout for forgeries. The GM rolls a secret Perception or Society check for you any time you encounter a forgery, even if you don't examine it closely.

# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Archetype (pistol-phenom)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Prerequisites**: Pistol Phenom Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, flourish

## Description

Your tricks discombobulate your opponent, leaving spots in their vision from the bright flash of your gunshot reflected straight into their eyes (or other visual organs). Attempt a performance save check against the target creature's Will DC and make a ranged firearm Strike against that target. If the Strike deals damage to the target creature and you succeed at the Performance check, the creature is Dazzled for 1 round.

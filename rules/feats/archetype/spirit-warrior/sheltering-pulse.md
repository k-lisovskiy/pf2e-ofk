## Description

You thrust your hand or weapon into the ground and release a pulse that creates a sheltering nexus of energy for you and your allies. Choose an unoccupied square within 15 feet. The nexus appears in a 15-foot type:emanation around that square and lasts for 3 rounds. You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to AC while in the area.

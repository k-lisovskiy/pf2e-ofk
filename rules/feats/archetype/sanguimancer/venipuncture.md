# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Archetype (sanguimancer)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Prerequisites**: Sanguimancer Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype

## Description

**Cost** all your remaining sanguimancy Hit Points (minimum 3)

---

A shell made up of hundreds of razor-thin needles of blood forms around you before exploding outward, lacerating foes in a 30-foot type:emanation. The needles deal piercing damage equal to double the number of sanguimancy Hit Points you spent, to a maximum of 80 damage. Creatures caught in the blast must attempt a reflex save save. The DC for this save is your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

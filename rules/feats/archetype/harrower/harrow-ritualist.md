# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Archetype (harrower)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Harrower Dedication
- **Special**: Can be taken up to 3 times.
- **Traits**: archetype

## Description

You immediately learn two of the following rituals: Astral Projection, Call Spirit, Commune, Commune, Collective Memories, or Binding Circle. When you perform any ritual, you can incorporate harrow cards into the ritual to gain a +2 status bonus to all skill checks made to resolve the effects of the ritual. You can take this feat more than once; each time you do, you learn two new rituals.

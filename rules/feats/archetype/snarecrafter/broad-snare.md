## Description

You can create longer snares for quick deployment, creating lines on the battlefield. When you prepare snares for quick deployment, you can prepare some of them as broad snares. A broad snare takes up a 20-foot type:line starting from the square in which you craft the snare but it costs two of your quick deployment snares. A broad snare can trigger from a creature entering any portion of its area, and all its effects apply over the full area.

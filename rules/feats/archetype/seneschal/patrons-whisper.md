# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Archetype (seneschal)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Seneschal Witch Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, auditory

## Description

You gain the ability to speak to your charge over great distances. You gain Message as an innate cantrip of your former patron's tradition. You can send a *message* to a creature regardless of range if they have ever been your charge. If you Lie as part of the message, the target receives a –2 circumstance penalty to their Perception DC and to Sense Motive checks to reveal the lie.

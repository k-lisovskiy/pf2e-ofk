# Overview

- **Prerequisites**: Lich Dedication, master in Intimidation
- **Traits**: archetype

## Description

You're surrounded by a palpable sense of menace and power that's terrifying to behold. The aura is a 15-foot emanation. An enemy that enters or ends its turn in the aura must attempt a will save save against the higher of your spell DC or class DC.

**Success** The creature is unaffected and temporarily immune to your Frightful Aura for 1 minute.

**Failure** The creature is Frightened 1.

**Critical Failure** The creature is Frightened 2.

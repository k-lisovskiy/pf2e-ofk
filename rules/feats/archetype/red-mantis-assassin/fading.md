# Overview

- **Level**: 10
- **Category**: Archetype (red-mantis-assassin)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Prerequisites**: Crimson Shroud
- **Traits**: archetype, teleportation

## Description

**Frequency** once per Crimson Shroud

**Trigger** You are targeted with a Strike by someone you can detect.

**Requirements** Your Crimson Shroud is active.

---

You partially fade from reality. The attacker must attempt a flat check (DC 11); on a failure, the attack passes harmlessly through the image of your body that remains behind. An instant later you return to reality, and your Crimson Shroud ends.

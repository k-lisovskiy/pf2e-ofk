# Overview

- **Level**: 6
- **Category**: Archetype (rivethun-invoker)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Frequency**: 1 per day
- **Prerequisites**: Rivethun Invoker Dedication
- **Traits**: archetype, healing

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** You fail or critically fail a saving throw against a curse, death, emotion, or fear effect.

---

You leverage your emotional turmoil to mend your physical form. You regain (2*(max(1,(@actor.system.skills.athletics.rank - 1))))d8 healing Hit Points. If you're a master in Athletics, you regain 4d8 Hit Points instead. If you're legendary in Athletics, you regain 6d8 Hit Points instead.

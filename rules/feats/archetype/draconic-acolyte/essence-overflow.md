## Description

**Requirements** You are Channeling Draconic Essence.

---

Uncontainable power explodes from your spectral dragon. Your Channel Draconic Essence ends, and all creatures (except you) in a 10-foot type:emanation from your spectral dragon take (5+floor((actor level - 4)/2))d6|options:area-damage damage with a reflex save save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. The damage's type matches the breath of your draconic benefactor. You then can't use Essence Overflow again for [[/r 1d4 #Recharge Essence Overflow]]{1d4 rounds}.

At 8th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.

- **Category**: Archetype (hellknight)

## Description

While moving on land, you ignore any difficult terrain caused by plants, fungi, or uneven ground. When you succeed at a skill check to Recall Knowledge about a creature with the animal, beast, fungus, or plant trait, you gain a +10-foot circumstance bonus to your Speeds for the rest of your turn.

## Description

You attempt to subdue a target with an attack and a follow‐up grab. Make a melee Strike. If it hits and deals damage, you can attempt an Athletics check to [[/act grapple]] the creature you hit. If you're wielding a flail or spiked chain, you can ignore Grapple's requirement that you have a hand free, and if you succeed at the Athletics check, you get a critical success instead. Both attacks count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until after you've made both of them.

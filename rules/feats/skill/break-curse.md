# Overview

- **Level**: 7
- **Category**: Skill

- **Prerequisites**: master in Occultism or Religion
- **Traits**: concentrate, exploration, healing, skill

## Description

You spend 8 hours praying or performing occult rites over the target, weakening a curse's power over them. Attempt to counteract the curse, using occultism save or religion save for your counteract check and half your level rounded up for the counteract rank. Break Curse only takes 10 minutes of prayer and rites if you are legendary in Occultism or Religion.

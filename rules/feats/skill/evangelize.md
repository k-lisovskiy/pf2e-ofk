- **Traits**: auditory, general, linguistic, mental, skill

## Description

You point out a detail that incontrovertibly supports your faith, causing a listener's mind to whirl. Attempt a diplomacy save check and compare the result to the Will DC of a single target that can hear you and understands your language; that target is then temporarily immune to Evangelize with respect to your deity or philosophy for 1 day. A creature that already agrees with you is unaffected, and at the GM's discretion, a target that genuinely changes its perspective to support your faith as a result of the argument is also otherwise unaffected.

---

**Critical Success** The target is Stupefied 2 for 1 round.

**Success** The target is Stupefied 1 for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is unaffected.

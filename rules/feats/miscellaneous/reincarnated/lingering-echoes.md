# Overview

- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Miscellaneous (reincarnated)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: reincarnated

## Description

Not everyone is reincarnated, and you feel a strong connection with the spiritual echoes of those who didn't receive the same opportunity as you. You gain Talking Corpse as an occult innate spell that you can cast once per day. You can ask a fourth question of a corpse when you cast this spell. The corpse attempts its will save save against your spell DC or your class DC, whichever is higher.

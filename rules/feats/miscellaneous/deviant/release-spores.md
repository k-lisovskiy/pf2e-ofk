# Overview

- **Level**: 2
- **Category**: Miscellaneous (deviant)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Traits**: deviant, fungus, magical, poison

## Description

You create a miasma of spores. These spores fill a 20-foot burst within 100 feet and remain for 1 round per level you have. All creatures within the spores become Concealed, and all creatures outside the spores become concealed to creatures within it. You can see through the spores without difficulty. You can Dismiss the spores.

**Awakening** The spores smell disgusting. Creatures in the area are Sickened 1 for as long as they remain in the area, plus 2 rounds.

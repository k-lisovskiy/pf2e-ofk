# Overview

- **Level**: 5
- **Category**: Ancestry (orc)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Orc Warmask
- **Traits**: orc

## Description

Your *warmask* sharpens your connection to the source of your mask's power. During your daily preparations, choose Fear, Phantom Pain, or Sure Strike.

Until your next daily preparations, you can cast this spell as a 1st-rank innate spell once per day; the spell's tradition is determined by the tradition tied to your *warmask*. You must be wearing your *warmask* to Cast the Spell.

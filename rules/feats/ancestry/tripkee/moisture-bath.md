## Description

---

Your moist skin is especially resistant to damage from temperature-altering effects. Attempt a flat check (DC 10); on a success you recover from any ongoing persistent fire and persistent cold damage. Additionally, you gain fire and cold resistance equal to half your level for 1 minute.

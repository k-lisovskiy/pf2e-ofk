# Overview

- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Ancestry (azarketi)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: azarketi

## Description

You can cast Cleanse Cuisine as a primal innate spell, but only on liquids. You can cast this spell once every 10 minutes.

You can also use your innate *cleanse cuisine* on a touched creature instead of a liquid to purify their blood, giving that creature a +2 status bonus for 1 minute on their ongoing saves against poisons already present in their body when you cast the spell.

Effect: Azarketi Purification

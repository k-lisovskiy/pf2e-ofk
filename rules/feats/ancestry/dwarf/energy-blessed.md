## Description

Energy runs through your blood more powerfully. When you use Energy Emanation, you can create an emanation of 5-foot emanation, 10-foot emanation, or 15-foot emanation. The damage increases to 6d6 plus an additional 1d6 for every level you have above 9th, instead of 1d6 plus an additional 1d6 for every 2 levels above 1st.

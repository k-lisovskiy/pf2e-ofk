- **Category**: Ancestry (dwarf)

## Description

**Access** You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

---

You're used to fighting and hiding in a haze of smoke and flame. You automatically succeed at the flat check (DC 5) to target a Concealed creature if that creature is concealed only by smoke. When you are concealed or Hidden in smoke, increase the DC of the flat check to target you to 6 if you're concealed or 12 if you're hidden.

# Overview

- **Level**: 1
- **Category**: Ancestry (dragonet)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: dragonet

## Description

You have a dragon's desire for treasure. You gain the Hefty Hauler skill feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Perception DC against attempts to [[/act steal]] from you and to any saving throw against an effect targeting an item you're holding or carrying.

At 11th level, you gain the Incredible Investiture general feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites.

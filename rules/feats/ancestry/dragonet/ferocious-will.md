# Overview

- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Ancestry (dragonet)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Frequency**: 1 per hour
- **Traits**: dragonet

## Description

**Frequency** once per hour

**Trigger** You succeed at a saving throw against a magical mental effect.

---

You send a blast of magical feedback at the effect's source, dealing ceil(actor level/2)d6 mental damage with a will save save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. If the target critically fails, it's also Slowed 1 for 1 round. At 11th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.

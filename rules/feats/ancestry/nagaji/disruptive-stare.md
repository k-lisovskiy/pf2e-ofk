# Overview

- **Level**: 13
- **Category**: Ancestry (nagaji)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Traits**: mental, nagaji, visual

## Description

**Trigger** A creature within 30 feet attempts to Cast a Spell with the concentrate trait.

---

Your frigid gaze can turn a foe's blood to ice. The triggering creature must attempt a will save save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. It then becomes temporarily immune for 24 hours.

---

**Failure** The triggering spell takes a –2 status penalty to its spell attack rolls and DCs.

**Critical Failure** The triggering spell is disrupted.

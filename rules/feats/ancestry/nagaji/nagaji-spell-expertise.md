## Description

Your magical skill rivals that of accomplished naga spellcasters. During your daily preparations, choose Flicker, Control Water, or Subconscious Suggestion. You can Cast this Spell as a 5th-rank occult innate spell once that day. You become an expert in occult spell DCs and occult spell attack rolls.

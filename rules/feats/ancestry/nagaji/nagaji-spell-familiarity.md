# Overview

- **Level**: 1
- **Category**: Ancestry (nagaji)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: nagaji

## Description

Either through study, exposure, or familial devotion, you have the magic of nagas bubbling in your blood. During your daily preparations, choose Daze, Detect Magic, or Telekinetic Hand. Until your next daily preparations, you can cast the chosen spell as an occult innate cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.

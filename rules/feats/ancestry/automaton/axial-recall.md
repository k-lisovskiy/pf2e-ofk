## Description

You have reconfigured the magical resonance of your core to attune to the planar energies of Axis, enabling instantaneous travel to that plane. You can cast Interplanar Teleport twice per week as an arcane innate spell that can only target yourself; you can travel only to Axis or the Universe, and your body serves as the focus component.

# Overview

- **Level**: 13
- **Category**: Ancestry (kitsune)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Star Orb
- **Traits**: kitsune

## Description

Your star orb hides a deadly miasma. You can cast 5th-rank Toxic Cloud as a divine innate spell once per day. The spell's normal components are replaced with a focus component (your star orb) and a somatic component to aim the orb towards the location for the spell.

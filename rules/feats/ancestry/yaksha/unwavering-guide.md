- **Category**: Ancestry (yaksha)
- **Action**: Passive

## Description

Sharp-eyed and sure-footed, your presence is a boon to those you guide. When you Scout, you grant your party a +2 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls instead of the normal +1. When your group uses the Hustle activity, they can use your Constitution modifier to determine how fast the group can Hustle together, even if it isn't the lowest in the group.

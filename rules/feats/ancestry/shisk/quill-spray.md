## Description

**Frequency** once per day

---

You can launch a mass of quills at opponents. You spray quills in a 30-foot cone, dealing (ceil(actor level/2))d8 piercing]|options:area-damage damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a reflex save saving throw against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. On a critical failure, a creature also takes (ceil(actor level/2)) bleed damage. At 11th level and every even level thereafter, the piercing damage increases by 1d8 and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.

---

***Note** The feat does not specify the type of saving throw required. A reflex saving throw has been added, in line with Barbed Quills.*

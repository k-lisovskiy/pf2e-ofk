# Overview

- **Level**: 5
- **Category**: Ancestry (gnome)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Illusion Sense
- **Traits**: gnome

## Description

By drawing upon the First World's magic, you can siphon a portion of that malleable world to create a convincing illusion. Once per day, you can cast Illusory Disguise, Item Facade, or Ventriloquism. At 7th level, the spell is heightened to 2nd rank, and every 2 levels thereafter, the spell is heightened an additional spell rank.

- **Category**: Ancestry (ghoran)

## Description

Certain magics in the world flow easily through your sap. You can cast Oaken Resilience and One with Plants as 2nd-rank arcane innate spells once per day each. A tree shape spell you cast this way transforms you into a Large flowering plant like a rosebush or lilac bush instead of a tree.

# Overview

- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Ancestry (ghoran)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Frequency**: 1 per hour
- **Traits**: disease, ghoran

## Description

**Frequency** once per hour

---

You issue a cloud of your fragrance like a full-body sneeze—but one that might save your life as the powerful perfume makes your attackers' eyes sting and water. Plants and fungi are immune to this effect, but all other creatures in a 10-foot emanation must attempt a fortitude save save against your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) with the following results.

---

**Critical Success** No effect.

**Success** The creature is Dazzled for 1 round.

**Failure** The creature is dazzled and can see only 10 feet away for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The creature is Blinded for 1 round.

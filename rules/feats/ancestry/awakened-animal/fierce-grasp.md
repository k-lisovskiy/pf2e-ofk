# Overview

- **Level**: 5
- **Category**: Ancestry (awakened-animal)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Prerequisites**: climbing animal heritage
- **Traits**: awakened-animal

## Description

**Requirements** You have an opponent Grabbed or Restrained.

---

Once you get your hands on someone, it's hard for them to get away. Your opponent takes a –2 circumstance penalty to their next attempt to Escape from being grabbed or restrained by you, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your AC against any attacks they make against you while you have them grabbed.

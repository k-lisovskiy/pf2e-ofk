# Overview

- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Ancestry (leshy)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Frequency**: 1 per hour
- **Traits**: leshy

## Description

**Frequency** once per hour

---

You unleash a cloud of pollen or similar spores. All creatures in a 10-foot emanation around you must attempt a fortitude save save using your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) with the following results. Plants and fungi are immune to this effect.

---

**Critical Success** No effect.

**Success** The creature is Dazzled for 1 round.

**Failure** The creature is dazzled and can see only 10 feet away for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The creature is Blinded for 1 round.

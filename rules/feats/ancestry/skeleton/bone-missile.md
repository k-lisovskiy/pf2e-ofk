## Description

You can remove your ribs to use them as arrows or bolts. When you draw a rib, you lose (ternary(gte(actor level,19),4,ternary(gte(actor level,12),3,2))) untyped HP, and the projectile deals 2 extra void damage if you Strike with it before the end of your next turn. The HP loss and extra damage both increase to 3 at 12th level and 4 at 19th level. Your rib cage magically replenishes, and any rib you draw crumbles to dust after being used for a Strike or at the end of your next turn, whichever comes first.

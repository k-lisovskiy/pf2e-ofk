## Description

In the darkness, your attacks can bring any red-faced giant to their knees. Once per hour, if you critically succeed at an attack roll against a Large or larger enemy and you're in an area of darkness, unseen hands reach from the shadows and pull the target to the ground, knocking them Prone.

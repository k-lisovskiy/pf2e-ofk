# Overview

- **Level**: 17
- **Category**: Ancestry (wayang)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Inherit the Dreaming Heirloom
- **Traits**: wayang

## Description

As you whisper to your pusaka, it attempts to cut away an enemy's shadow, leaving them in an eternal sleep. Your pusaka gains the following Activation.

**Activate—Cut the Shadow** 2 actions (concentrate, manipulate, mental)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** Your pusaka becomes a kris made of darkness and drives itself into an adjacent enemy's shadow. That enemy takes 80 mental damage with a fortitude save save against your spell DC or class DC, whichever is higher. If this reduces the creature to 0 Hit Points, the target doesn't die, but its shadow is severed from its body, becoming a Shadow Spawn that attempts to flee from the target's body. As long as the shadow spawn exists, the target remains in a dreamless slumber; if destroyed, the target regains its shadow and awakens (if its body was kept properly while Unconscious).

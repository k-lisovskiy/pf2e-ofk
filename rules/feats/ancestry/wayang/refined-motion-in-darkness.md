- **Category**: Ancestry (wayang)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: wayang

## Description

The ability to move elegantly and undetected through darkness aided your people as they fled the Netherworld. You can move 5 feet farther when you take the Sneak action, up to your Speed. In addition, as long as you continue to use Sneak actions and succeed at your Stealth check, you don't become observed if you end a Sneak action in dim light or darkness, as long as you have cover or greater cover or are Concealed at the end of your turn.

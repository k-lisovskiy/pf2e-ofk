# Overview

- **Level**: 13
- **Category**: Ancestry (versatile-heritages)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Prerequisites**: Beastbrood
- **Traits**: nephilim

## Description

**Requirements** You're holding something sacred or precious to a creature, such as a relic or personal depiction of their deity or a precious personal memento.

---

You brandish the precious object you're holding menacingly, at once mocking it and threatening its safety. Attempt to Demoralize the creature whose precious item you threaten. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the Intimidation check to Demoralize, and on a success, the creature is Off-Guard for 1 round out of distraction for the object's safety and is Stupefied 1 for 1 minute (or stupefied 2 on a critical success). These conditions are both in addition to the normal effects of Demoralize. The creature is then temporarily immune to any Idol Threat using that specific object for 1 day.

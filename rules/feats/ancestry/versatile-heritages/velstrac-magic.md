## Description

You know there's so much more to sensation than whips and chains—but whips and chains also have their place. You can cast Telekinetic Maneuver and Web once per day each as 2nd-rank divine innate spells; in both cases, the spells manifest shadowy chains rather than their usual visual signifiers.

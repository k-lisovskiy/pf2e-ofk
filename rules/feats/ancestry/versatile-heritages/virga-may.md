- **Special**: Can only be selected at 1st level.

## Description

Born of a storm hag, you have one crystal-blue or rain-gray eye. You share your mother's power over wind and storm. You can cast Electric Arc as an occult innate cantrip. As normal, a cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.

# Overview

- **Level**: 17
- **Category**: Ancestry (versatile-heritages)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: reflection

## Description

You create a copy of a foe by calling them forth from a nearby reflection, clonally growing them in an instant from one of your hairs or otherwise creating them in a way that matches your own nature as a reflection. You can cast Duplicate Foe once per day as an innate occult spell. At 20th level, your innate *duplicate foe* is heightened to 8th rank.

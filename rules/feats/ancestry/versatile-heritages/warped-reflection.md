# Overview

- **Level**: 1
- **Category**: Ancestry (versatile-heritages)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Frequency**: 1 per minute
- **Traits**: concentrate, emotion, fear, mental, reflection, visual

## Description

**Frequency** once per minute

---

Due to your origins, your form is more flexible than most. You shift your appearance to resemble a creature within 30 feet but with an unsettling, horrifying twist. Though this transformation is momentary, witnessing it gives the target deep existential dread. It must succeed at a will save save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC or become Frightened 1 (Frightened 2 on a critical failure) and be Stupefied 1 as long as the frightened condition lasts. Once you've used Warped Reflection against a creature, it's temporarily immune for 24 hours.

# Overview

- **Level**: 13
- **Category**: Ancestry (versatile-heritages)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: beastkin

## Description

You can share your power with others, but they can't escape the werecreature's curse as easily as you can. You can cast a 5th-rank Moon Frenzy as a primal innate spell once per day. At 15th level, you cast a 6th-rank *moon frenzy* instead. At 17th level, your 6th-rank *moon frenzy* grants its standard effects, except the temporary Hit Points increase to 15 and the silver weakness increases to 15. At 20th level, you cast a 10th-rank *moon frenzy* instead. The spell uses your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

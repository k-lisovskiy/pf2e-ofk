- **Prerequisites**: Proteankin

## Description

You have a powerful creative drive that is clear to any who interact with you. You are trained in Performance. If you were already trained in Performance (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the Impressive Performance skill feat, and when you use Performance [[/act make-an-impression variant=performance]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the check.

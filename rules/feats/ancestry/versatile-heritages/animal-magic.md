# Overview

- **Category**: Ancestry (versatile-heritages)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: beastkin

## Description

Your shapeshifting grants you a magical connection to the animal world. You can cast Animal Messenger, Calm (animals only), and Speak with Animals as 2nd-rank primal innate spells once per day each. These spells use your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

## Description

You have power connected to the powerful jann amirs. You can cast Read Omens and 4th-rank Invisibility each once per day as arcane innate spells, and you can cast Detect Magic at will as an arcane innate cantrip, heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.

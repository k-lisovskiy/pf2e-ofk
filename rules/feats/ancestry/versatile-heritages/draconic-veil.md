# Overview

- **Level**: 13
- **Category**: Ancestry (versatile-heritages)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: dragonblood

## Description

Like some dragons, your forebear had the supernatural ability to change their shape to walk among humanoids. You have learned how to mimic this ability. You can cast Humanoid Form as a 5th-rank innate spell once per day, except that the duration is increased to 1 hour. The spell's tradition matches your draconic exemplar's.

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

---

You tap into the collective consciousness of the Godmind, the governing entity of axiomite society, to rapidly parse near-infinite variables to read your surroundings. For 1 minute, you gain precise motion sense out to 60 feet, you can see Invisible creatures and objects, and you gain the benefits of Truesight (with a counteract check modifier equal to your Perception modifier).

# Overview

- **Category**: Ancestry (versatile-heritages)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Special**: Can only be selected at 1st level.
- **Traits**: lineage, naari

## Description

The fire inside you asserts itself as beaming, radiant light. Your body is naturally luminescent, glowing with the effects of a primal Light cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. The light is involuntary and constant; if counteracted, it returns in [[/gmr 1d4 #rounds]]{1d4 rounds}. You suffer a -2 circumstance penalty on Stealth checks to Hide or Sneak and gain a +1 circumstance bonus on saves against light effects and effects that inflict the Blinded or Dazzled conditions.

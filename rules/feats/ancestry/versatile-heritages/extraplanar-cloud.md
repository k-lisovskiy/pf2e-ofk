# Overview

- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Ancestry (versatile-heritages)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Extraplanar Haze
- **Traits**: nephilim

## Description

When you use Extraplanar Haze, you can spend 1 additional action to disperse the particles as a 10-foot type:emanation cloud centered on you instead of as a haze; the activity gains the concentrate trait. All creatures within the cloud become Concealed, and all creatures outside the cloud become concealed to creatures within it.

# Overview

- **Level**: 1
- **Category**: Ancestry (shared-ancestry-feats)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: ardande, naari, oread, suli, sylph, talos, undine

## Description

You've trained with weapons used by your genie ancestors. You are trained with Falchions, Ranseurs, Scimitars, and Tridents.

In addition, you gain access to all uncommon geniekin weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial geniekin weapons are simple weapons and advanced geniekin weapons are martial weapons.

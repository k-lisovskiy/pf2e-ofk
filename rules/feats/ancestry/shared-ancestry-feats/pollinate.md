# Overview

- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Ancestry (shared-ancestry-feats)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Prerequisites**: ardande trait, plant trait, or wood trait

## Description

**Trigger** You take bludgeoning damage.

---

As you're hit, you emit a dense cloud of pollen, obscuring you from sight. You become Concealed until the beginning of your next turn.

---

**Special** This feat gains the trait for your ancestry.

## Description

Whether it's because you play especially vigorously or are simply off-key, the music of your belly drum can physically wound your foes. You deal (ceil(actor level/2))d4 sonic]|options:area-damage damage to all creatures in a 30-foot type:cone, with a fortitude save save equal to your spell DC or class DC, whichever is higher. Such vigorous drumming does leave your belly a bit sore, though, preventing you from using this ability again for [[/r 1d4 #rounds]]{1d4 rounds}.

At 15th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d4.

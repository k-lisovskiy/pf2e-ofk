- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Ancestry (tanuki)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Prerequisites**: Teakettle Form

## Description

**Trigger** You Drop Prone or otherwise become Prone.

---

As you fall to the ground, you transform into a white bottle, gourd, or sake jar, then roll away before anyone can pick you up. You Crawl up to two times, and your movement doesn't provoke reactions—you somehow always roll just out of reach. When you come to a stop, you return to your tanuki form, face down on the ground.

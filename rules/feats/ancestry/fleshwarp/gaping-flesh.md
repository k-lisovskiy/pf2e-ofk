# Overview

- **Level**: 5
- **Category**: Ancestry (fleshwarp)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Frequency**: 1 per day
- **Traits**: fleshwarp

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** A creature you are aware of damages you with a melee Strike.

---

Your wound yawns open, appalling your attacker. The attacker must succeed at a fortitude save saving throw against your class or spell DC, whichever is higher, or become Sickened 1 (Sickened 2 on a critical failure). It can't recover from the sickened condition while it's adjacent to you.

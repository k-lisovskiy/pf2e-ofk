# Overview

- **Level**: 17
- **Category**: Ancestry (athamaru)
- **Action**: Reaction
- **Traits**: athamaru

## Description

**Trigger** You are Hidden and a creature who hasn't detected you passes within 20 feet of you.

---

Like the moray eels athamaru communities raise and train, you know how to quickly strike from hiding. Stride or Swim directly toward the triggering creature and make a melee Strike against it. The target creature is Off-Guard to this attack.

---

**Special** If you're mounted on an eel, you can have the eel move instead of yourself.

# Overview

- **Level**: 1
- **Category**: Ancestry (athamaru)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Frequency**: 1 per day
- **Traits**: athamaru, inhaled, poison

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

---

While athamarus' natural pheromones are typically used for communication, you have developed yours into a defense mechanism to ward off foes. You emit a thick spray of defensive pheromones at an adjacent creature, who takes (floor((actor level +1)/2)+1)d6 poison damage with a fortitude save save against your class DC.

At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.

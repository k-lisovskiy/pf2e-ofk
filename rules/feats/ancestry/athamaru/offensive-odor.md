## Description

You've mastered your defensive pheromones and have nearly complete control over them. When you Emit Defensive Odor, you can either choose to increase the damage dealt to a single target by changing the damage dice to d8s ((floor((actor level +1)/2)+1)d8 poison]|options:area-damage), or leave the damage unchanged but spray your pheromones in a 15-foot cone.

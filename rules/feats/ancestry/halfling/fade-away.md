# Overview

- **Level**: 9
- **Category**: Ancestry (halfling)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Easily Dismissed
- **Traits**: halfling

## Description

Your ability to blend into the background allows you to fade away entirely or appear innocuous even to magical effects. You gain Invisibility and Disguise Magic as 2nd-rank innate occult spells. You can target only yourself with *invisibility*, and you must be the primary target of *disguise magic*. You can cast each spell once per day.

## Description

**Access** Tian Xia origin, or you're a follower of Sangpotshi.

---

Once per year, when you die, you turn into an immaterial ball of light. After a week, you return to life with the effects of a successful (but not critically successful) 9th-rank Reincarnate ritual. You always reincarnate into a sprite, but you *reincarnate* as a randomly selected sprite heritage.

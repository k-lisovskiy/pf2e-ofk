# Overview

- **Level**: 13
- **Category**: Ancestry (yaoguai)
- **Action**: 2 actions
- **Frequency**: 1 per day
- **Traits**: auditory, emotion, fear, mental, yaoguai

## Description

**Frequency** once per day

**Requirements** You're in yaoguai form.

---

You let out a mighty howl, chant of death, or speak with eerie repetition that plants fear in others. All enemies within 30 feet must attempt a will save save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is Frightened 1.

**Failure** The target is frightened 1 and Off-Guard.

**Critical Failure** The target is Frightened 2 and off-guard.

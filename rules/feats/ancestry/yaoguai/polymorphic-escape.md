## Description

**Trigger** You would become Grabbed, Immobilized, Prone, or Restrained.

---

You polymorph into a tiny vermin, a cloud of leaves, or another shape that allows you to escape. Attempt a flat check (DC 16); on a success, you ignore the triggering effect and can Step. You then return to your humanoid shape.

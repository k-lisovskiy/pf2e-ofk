## Description

You can transform into a small animal or celestial creature to scurry and spy. You can Change Shape to assume a crawling form. While in crawling form, you gain the effect of a 1st-rank Pest Form, except you can transform only into a shape matching your heritage; for example, if you were once were a wolf, you turn into a wolf puppy.

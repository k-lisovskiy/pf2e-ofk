## Description

**Access** Varisian ethnicity or New Thassilon nationality

---

You have tattoos on your body corresponding to one of the ancient Thassilonian schools of magic. Choose one of the following schools of magic: abjuration (Shield), conjuration (Tangle Vine), enchantment (Daze), evocation (Electric Arc), illusion (Ghost Sound), necromancy (Void Warp), or transmutation (Sigil). You can cast the associated cantrip (listed in parentheses) as an innate arcane spell at will.

# Overview

- **Level**: 5
- **Category**: Ancestry (human)
- **Action**: Passive
- **Traits**: human

## Description

Like many humans raised in a close-knit community, you have always been strongly attuned to the presence of others. Willing allies that you are aware of within 60 feet that would otherwise be Undetected by you are instead Hidden from you. The flat check for you to target willing allies within 60 feet that are hidden from you is flat check (DC 5) instead of 11.

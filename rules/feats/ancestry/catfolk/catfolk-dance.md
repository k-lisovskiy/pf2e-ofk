# Overview

- **Level**: 1
- **Category**: Ancestry (catfolk)
- **Action**: 1 action
- **Traits**: catfolk

## Description

You have a habit of always being in the way when other creatures attempt to move. Attempt an acrobatics save check against an adjacent creature's Reflex DC.

Effect: Catfolk Dance

---

**Critical Success** The target creature gains a -2 circumstance penalty to Reflex saves and is Off-Guard until the start of your next turn.

**Success** The target creature gains a -2 circumstance penalty to Reflex saves until the start of your next turn.

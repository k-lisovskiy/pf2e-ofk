- **Action**: Passive

## Description

Memories of your first life are strong and continually return to you, even after several incarnations. You gain the Adopted Ancestry feat and can choose any common or uncommon humanoid ancestry with the feat. You also gain the Additional Lore general feat for your chosen ancestry's Lore, such as Dwarven Lore or Ratfolk Lore.

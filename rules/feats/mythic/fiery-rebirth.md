# Overview

- **Level**: 4
- **Category**: Mythic
- **Action**: Free Action
- **Traits**: fire, healing, mythic

## Description

**Trigger** You regain Hit Points while you are Unconscious.

---

When you escape death, you return to life with retributive fire that burns those who harmed you. You expend 1 Mythic Point, return to consciousness, Stand, and regain additional Hit Points equal to your level. You then erupt in flames, dealing fire damage equal to twice your level ((2*actor level) fire) to adjacent foes, with a reflex save save against your class DC. Foes that critically fail the save are also pushed 5 feet away from you.

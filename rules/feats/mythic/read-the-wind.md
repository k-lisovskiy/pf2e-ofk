- **Level**: 4
- **Category**: Mythic
- **Action**: Passive
- **Prerequisites**: Hunter's Calling
- **Traits**: mythic

## Description

You can derive meaning from the tiniest details when tracking an enemy. Whenever you succeed at a check to Track at mythic proficiency, you can immediately attempt a check to Recall Knowledge about the creature you're tracking, also at mythic proficiency and with a suitable skill determined by the GM.

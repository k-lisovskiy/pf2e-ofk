## Description

You've become a favorite of Sorshen, the Runelord of Lust. She watches over you wherever you go, protecting you and your companions in times of need. The first time each day you either cast or are the target of a spell of 6th rank or higher that has the emotion or healing trait, you gain 1 Mythic Point. You gain the Sorshen's Devotion reaction.

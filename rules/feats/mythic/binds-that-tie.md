## Description

You excel at assisting in capturing your foes. When you attempt a check to Grapple an opponent who's already Grabbed by an ally, you can spend a Mythic Point to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. On a success, you deal additional bludgeoning damage to the target equal to twice your Strength modifier.

## Description

You bombard a target with distracting auditory illusions, surrounding them with a tumultuous cacophony of overlapping speech, whispers, screams, and muttering. The target is Off-Guard, takes a -2 status penalty to purely auditory Perception checks, and must succeed at a flat check (DC 5) to use auditory abilities or verbal spell components. Similarly, a creature who attempts to affect the target with an auditory effect must succeed at a flat check (DC 5) or the attempt fails. The duration depends on the target's Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The duration is 1 round.

**Failure** The duration is 3 rounds.

**Critical Failure** The duration is 1 minute.

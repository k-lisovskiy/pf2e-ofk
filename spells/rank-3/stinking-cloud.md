## Description

You create a cloud of putrid mist in the area. The cloud functions as Mist except it sickens creatures that end their turns within the cloud. (The Concealed condition is not a poison effect.)

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is Sickened 1.

**Failure** The creature is Sickened 1 and Slowed 1 while in the cloud.

**Critical Failure** The creature is Sickened 2 and Slowed 1 until it leaves the cloud.

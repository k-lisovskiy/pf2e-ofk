## Description

You conjure an arrow of acid that keeps corroding the target after it hits. Make a spell attack against the target. On a hit, you deal 3d8 acid damage plus (floor(rank/2))d6 persistent acid damage. On a critical hit, double the initial damage, but not the persistent damage.

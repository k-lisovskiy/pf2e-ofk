## Description

You cut your own body in a ritualistic manner, causing similar damage to a creature in your line of sight. These cuts are superficial and cause 1d4 slashing damage to you; however, the wounds that open up on our target's body are far deeper. The targeted creature takes 5d8 slashing damage; a creature that critically fails this saving throw also takes (1d8 + level -4) bleed damage. If you cast this as a three-action spell, the spell instead affects a 5-foot burst.

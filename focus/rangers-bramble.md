# Overview

- **Rank**: 3 (focus spell)
- **Cast**: 2
- **Range**: 100 feet
- **Area**: 5-foot burst
- **Defense**: reflex
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Traits**: concentrate, focus, manipulate, plant, ranger

## Description

You cause plants in the area to entangle your foes, with the effects of Entangling Flora. A creature that critically fails the save takes (max(0,level -1))d4 bleed damage in addition to being Immobilized. Escaping your bramble doesn't end the bleed damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The bleed damage on a critical failure increases by 1d4.

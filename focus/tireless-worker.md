# Overview

- **Rank**: 4 (focus spell)
- **Cast**: 2
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: 1 creature
- **Duration**: 1 minute
- **Traits**: cleric, concentrate, focus, healing, manipulate

## Description

You suppress your choice of one of the following conditions that's affecting the target: Clumsy, Encumbered, Enfeebled, or Fatigued. The duration of the condition doesn't elapse while it is suppressed, so if you don't remove the effect that caused the condition, the condition returns after the spell's duration expires.

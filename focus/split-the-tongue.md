# Overview

- **Rank**: 1 (focus spell)
- **Cast**: reaction
- **Range**: 30 feet
- **Targets**: A creature within 30 feet fails a Deception or Diplomacy check.
- **Defense**: basic fortitude
- **Traits**: focus, manipulate

## Description

The deceiving creature's tongue splits like a serpent's tongue. The creature takes 1d8 slashing damage and must attempt a Fortitude save.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is Stupefied 1 for [[/r 1d4]]{1d4 rounds}.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double damage and is Stupefied 2 for [[/r 1d4]]{1d4 rounds}.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
